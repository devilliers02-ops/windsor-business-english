Privacy policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026 · Version 1.1

Windsor Business English (“Windsor”, “we”) is responsible for the personal data described here. We handle it in line with Law No. 2013-450 of 19 June 2013 on the protection of personal data in Côte d’Ivoire, which is supervised by the ARTCI.

What we collect

  • Messages you send us. When you tap a WhatsApp or email button, the message is prepared on your device and sent through WhatsApp or your email app. We receive your name, number or address, and what you write.
  • Learner portal profile. The email and password you use to open the practice portal, and your quiz scores. In the current version these stay in your own browser. They are not sent to our servers.
  • Level check and practice quizzes. Answers and scores are calculated in your browser. We only see a result if you choose to share it with us on WhatsApp.
  • Feedback. Your testimonial is prepared on your device. Nothing reaches us until you send it, and we publish it only with your consent.
  • Preferences. Your language and day or night mode are saved on your device.
  • Technical data. The services that deliver this page (see the cookies policy) can see your IP address and browser details when you load it.
  • Anonymous visit statistics. We use GoatCounter, a privacy-focused service, to count visits, countries, pages viewed, buttons tapped and how long the page stays open. It uses no cookies, does not follow you across other websites and does not build profiles of individual visitors. It does not run if your browser sends a Do Not Track signal.
  • If you become a learner: your name, contact details, employer, job role, sector, English level, attendance, progress and payment records.

Who sees it

We do not sell personal data. Access is limited to Windsor staff and coaches who need it, bound by confidentiality. We use service providers for messaging (WhatsApp, email), website hosting, and payments. Each may only use data to provide its service to us.

If your employer pays for your training, we share your attendance, level and progress with them, as set out in their agreement with us. We do not share your private messages or the detail of your mistakes. We tell you before your programme starts.

Transfers outside Côte d’Ivoire

Some providers, such as WhatsApp, email and cloud services, process data outside Côte d’Ivoire. We choose providers that protect data to a comparable standard and, where the law requires it, we make the declaration or obtain the authorisation from the ARTCI.

Your rights

You can ask to see the data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, object to its use, or withdraw a consent you gave. You can refuse marketing at any time. Write to us and we will answer within one month. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the ARTCI.

Security

We limit access, use encrypted connections, and store passwords only in hashed form. On a shared or public computer, log out of the portal and clear the site data in your browser, because the current portal profile lives in that browser.

Children

Our services are for working adults aged 18 or over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you think we have, tell us and we will delete it.

Enrolment and payment terms

Last updated: 6 October 2026 · Version 1.0

These terms apply to every Windsor programme, workshop and coaching package, together with the quote you accept.

Quote and enrolment

  • Fees are given in a written quote, in CFA francs (XOF) unless the quote says otherwise. A quote is valid for 30 days.
  • Enrolment is confirmed when you accept the quote and make the first payment it asks for.
  • Taxes are shown separately where they apply.
  • You can pay by the methods listed on the quote, such as bank transfer or mobile money. We issue a receipt or invoice for every payment.

Placement

The free level check and a short conversation with a coach place you in a CEFR-aligned group or track. Your coach may move you to a more suitable group in the first two sessions.

Sessions and attendance

  • Sessions start and end at the times agreed. A late arrival does not extend the session.
  • You can move an individual coaching session if you tell us at least 24 hours before. Group sessions cannot be moved, and we will point you to the practice material for a missed one.
  • If Windsor cancels or moves a session, we will give you a replacement session.
  • We do not record sessions unless everyone present agrees first.

Certificates and exams

A certificate of completion is issued when you attend at least 80% of the sessions. Exam fees for TOEIC, TOEFL, BULATS or Cambridge BEC are paid separately to the exam body. We prepare you for these exams but we cannot promise a particular score or level.

Companies and sponsors

When an organisation pays for learners, it is responsible for the fees under the quote. Learners are told what will be reported to the sponsor, as set out in our privacy policy. Group programmes run with the group size stated in the quote.

Late payment

If an invoice is more than 14 days overdue, we may pause access to sessions and the portal until it is paid. We will warn you first.

Materials and unforeseen events

Course materials are for your own use and may not be shared outside your organisation. Neither side is responsible for a delay caused by events outside its control, such as a power or network failure. We will agree a new date promptly.

Windsor AI tutor policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026 · Version 1.0

Windsor is our AI practice tutor for Business English. It helps with vocabulary, emails, presentations and practice quizzes.

What the Windsor AI tutor is today

On this website, the AI tutor chat is an example conversation and the portal quiz uses fixed questions. Neither sends what you type to an AI provider. If we connect the AI tutor to a generative AI service, we will update this policy first and tell learners before it goes live.

What to expect

  • You will always know you are talking to an AI, never a person.
  • The AI tutor can make mistakes. Check anything important with your coach or a trusted source.
  • The AI tutor supports learning. It does not give legal, medical or financial advice.
  • The AI tutor does not decide your placement, grade or certificate. Coaches make those decisions.
  • Levels suggested by the AI tutor or the level check are indicative only.

Your data when generative AI is switched on

  • Do not enter confidential information such as client names, contracts, passwords, health or financial details.
  • We will not allow your conversations to be used to train public AI models.
  • Any provider we use will be bound by a contract that limits its use of your data, and we will say how long conversations are kept.
  • A member of staff may review conversations to check quality and safety. We will tell you when that applies.

Reporting a problem

If the AI tutor gives an answer that is wrong, unfair or inappropriate, tell us by email or WhatsApp with a screenshot if you can. We review reports and correct the content.

Learner conduct and safeguarding

Last updated: 6 October 2026 · Version 1.0

This applies to learners, coaches and staff, in the classroom, on Zoom, on WhatsApp and in the learner portal.

What we expect

  • Treat everyone with respect. We do not accept harassment, bullying, discrimination or threats of any kind.
  • Keep what colleagues share in class confidential, especially in company groups.
  • Do not record, photograph or share a session without everyone’s agreement.
  • Be on time and ready to take part. Use group chats for learning and not for advertising or unrelated messages.

Safeguarding

  • Our programmes are for adults aged 18 or over.
  • Coaches are recruited with references and a check of their qualifications.
  • One-to-one coaching takes place online or on premises where others can be reached, and coaches contact learners only through Windsor’s official channels.
  • Coaches and staff may not ask learners for gifts, favours or money outside the agreed fees.

Raising a concern

If you feel unsafe or see behaviour that worries you, write to us by email or WhatsApp. We keep reports confidential, act quickly, and do not allow anyone to be penalised for reporting in good faith. If someone is in immediate danger, contact the local emergency services first.

If the rules are broken

Depending on how serious it is, we may give a warning, suspend a person from sessions, or end their participation. In serious cases the refund policy may not apply. The person concerned can respond first, and can use our complaints policy.

Accessibility statement

Last updated: 6 October 2026 · Version 1.0

We want everyone to be able to use this website and our lessons. We aim to meet WCAG 2.2 level AA.

What is in place

  • The site works with a keyboard, has a skip-to-content link and shows a clear focus outline.
  • It is available in English and French, in day and night modes.
  • Animations respect the “reduce motion” setting of your device, and the animated prepositions can be paused.
  • Quiz answers and results are announced to screen readers, and controls carry text labels.
  • The layout adapts to phones, tablets and computers, and to larger text.

Known limits

  • The free level check has a 3-minute countdown. If a time limit is a barrier for you, ask for an untimed version.
  • News articles open on publishers’ sites, whose accessibility we do not control.
  • This statement is based on our own review in October 2026. It has not been audited by an outside body.

Asking for an adjustment

If you find a barrier on the site or in a lesson, or need an adjustment such as extra time, large print or a different format, tell us by email or WhatsApp. We will reply within 5 working days and agree a solution with you.
