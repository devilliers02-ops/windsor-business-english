About us

We exist to give African professionals a global voice

Windsor Business English is a specialist institute in Abidjan. We teach one thing, Business English, and we teach it well.

Across West Africa, talented professionals lose opportunities, not because of their skills but because of the language they must present them in. Windsor was created to close that gap.

We are not a general language school. Every lesson, exercise and coach is focused on the English you need to negotiate, present, write and lead.

Evergreen guides

Emails

5 phrases that make your emails sound senior

Small changes in wording change how colleagues and clients see you.

Read more

Swap “I want” for “I would like to”. Use “Further to our call” instead of “About our call”. Write “Could you please confirm” instead of “Tell me”. Close with “I look forward to your reply” and sign off with “Kind regards”. Keep one request per paragraph.

3 min read
Grammar

In, on, at: prepositions in meetings

The three small words behind the most mistakes at work.

Read more

Use “at” for clock times (at 9 a.m.), “on” for days and dates (on Monday, on 5 May) and “in” for months, years and longer periods (in June, in 2026). In meetings: “at the meeting”, “on the agenda”, “in the report”.

2 min read
Presentations

Open any presentation with the PREP framework

Point, Reason, Example, Point: a structure you can use in 60 seconds.

Read more

State your Point first (“We should move to a hybrid model”). Give your Reason (“It cuts costs and keeps clients close”). Add an Example (“Our pilot saved 18%”). Close by repeating the Point. It works for updates, pitches and unexpected questions.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Who is Windsor?

Windsor is our AI tutor: a practice partner available at any hour for vocabulary, email writing and presentation skills in your sector.

Do I need a certain English level to start?

No. We begin with a free level check and place you on the right CEFR track, from beginner to advanced.

Can my company enrol a whole team?

Yes. Our B2B programmes are built around your teams and your industry. Message us and the management team will reply with a proposal.

Are classes online or in person?

Both. Windsor is hybrid: live coaching in Abidjan or online, plus daily practice in the learner portal.

How much do the programmes cost?

Fees depend on the programme, the group size and the format, so we quote on request. Message us on WhatsApp with your role and goal and we will reply with a clear quote and a free level check.

How can I pay?

You receive a written quote in CFA francs (XOF). You can pay by bank transfer or mobile money, and we issue a receipt or invoice for every payment. Companies and sponsors pay under the quote.

Do you offer small groups?

Yes, on request. Groups usually have 8 to 12 people, but we can run a small group of 4 to 6 for more individual attention.
