# Windsor Business English

Static website for Windsor Business English, Abidjan. Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, no build step.

- `index.html`: the website
- `policies.html`: legal and policy pages
- `news.json`: stories shown in the Daily Business English brief
- `data.js`, `programmes.js`: content; `data.js` also holds the social links (`SOCIAL`) and proof figures (`PROOF`)
- `styles.css`, `app.js`, `lang.js`: design and behaviour (EN/FR switching lives in `lang.js`)
